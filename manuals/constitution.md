CONSTITUTION

Article I — Mission
The objective of this portfolio is not to maximize returns in every market cycle. The objective is to maximize long-term compounded wealth by minimizing major drawdowns and avoiding prolonged recovery periods. Capital preservation during unfavorable macro regimes is valued more highly than capturing every percentage point of upside.
 
Article II — Philosophy
Markets move in regimes. No single asset class outperforms across all macro environments. The portfolio therefore separates:
Asset allocation (when to own equities)
Security selection (what equities to own)
These are independent decisions.
 
Article III — Master Rule
The KISS Model is the portfolio’s master asset allocation signal. All other analysis exists to inform portfolio construction, not to override KISS. It determines whether equities deserve capital. Individual opinions, news, valuation, forecasts, emotions and market narratives do not override KISS.
Article IV — Portfolio Construction
The portfolio exists to identify the highest-conviction equity investments before capital is deployed. Portfolio construction and macro allocation are independent disciplines. Portfolio construction determines what deserves to be owned. KISS determines when those assets deserve capital. When KISS authorizes equity exposure, capital shall be allocated only to investments that satisfy the portfolio’s long-term investment principles, including:
durable business quality;
rational capital allocation;
sustainable shareholder returns;
broad diversification across businesses, sectors and geographies;
selective exposure to long-term structural growth themes;
simplicity, transparency and low turnover.
Every holding must have a durable investment thesis that remains valid across multiple market cycles.
 
Article V — Drawdown Philosophy
The portfolio does not seek:
tops
bottoms
perfect timing
Instead, the portfolio accepts:
imperfect exits
imperfect entries
false signals
 
Avoiding the bulk of major bear markets while participating in the bulk of major bull markets. Missing portions of advances is considered an acceptable cost of avoiding prolonged capital destruction.
 
Article VI — Compounding Principle
Participating in a recovery from a stronger capital base is superior to waiting for a recovery from a weaker one. The portfolio therefore prioritizes avoiding large drawdowns over maximizing participation in every rally.
 
Article VII — Risk Management
Risk management always precedes return maximization. No investment idea is sufficiently attractive to override the portfolio’s macro risk framework.
 
Article VIII — Behaviour
During market stress: No discretionary macro forecasts shall override KISS. During euphoric markets: Fear of missing out shall not override KISS. The purpose of the system is to remove prediction, discretion and emotion from asset allocation decisions.
 
Article IX — Process
Every investment decision answers two independent questions:
Should equities be owned? Answered solely by KISS.
Which equities should be owned? Answered by portfolio research. 
Neither question substitutes for the other.
 
Article X — Review
The Constitution may be amended only:
outside periods of market stress
outside periods of market euphoria
after careful review
never because of recent performance.
 
Article XI — Long-Term Objective
Success is measured by:
superior long-term compound growth
disciplined execution
avoidance of catastrophic drawdowns
faithful adherence to the investment process
consistent application of risk management
 
Article XII — Process Before Prediction
The portfolio makes no attempt to determine whether the current market resembles 1998, 2000, 2008, or any other historical period. Such judgments are unknowable in real time. The portfolio therefore follows a predefined process rather than attempting to forecast turning points.
