# DAILY MARKET STATE — GOVERNING INSTRUCTIONS

## 1. PURPOSE

The Daily Market State system is the portfolio's standardized fast-moving
market-observation framework.

Its purpose is to:

- measure current market conditions consistently;
- take the market's daily "temperature";
- identify persistent changes in breadth, leadership, credit, rates,
  financial conditions, factors, sectors and cross-asset behavior;
- detect a small number of predefined, economically meaningful market patterns;
- distinguish persistent trends from one-day noise;
- compare current market behavior with the standing Macro Narrative;
- evaluate relevance to the actual portfolio, its exposures, theses and risks;
- create a repeatable historical dataset that allows observations to be
  compared apples-to-apples over time;
- support a concise but sufficiently detailed morning market briefing.

The Daily Market State system is NOT:

- a discretionary market forecast;
- a market-top or market-bottom model;
- an independent asset-allocation model;
- a replacement for KISS;
- a substitute for the Macro Narrative;
- a mechanical trading signal;
- a news summary;
- a mechanism for inventing patterns after observing market moves.

The objective is observation, measurement, comparison and pattern recognition.

Do not imply greater predictive power than the evidence supports.


## 2. STANDARDIZATION PRINCIPLE

Every Daily Market State observation must use the same:

- observation timing;
- input categories;
- source hierarchy;
- chart universe;
- ratio orientation;
- watchlist-column interpretation;
- scoring definitions;
- pattern definitions;
- terminology;
- historical comparison methodology;
- output structure.

Historical comparability takes priority over narrative creativity.

Do not change interpretation rules because a particular market move appears
important.

Do not selectively emphasize indicators merely because they support the
preferred narrative.

Patterns must be identified from predefined conditions, not invented
retrospectively.

The purpose of standardization is to allow genuine changes and persistent
patterns to emerge from repeated observations rather than being imagined
through discretionary interpretation.


## 3. SYSTEM HIERARCHY

Portfolio Constitution
→ KISS
→ Macro Narrative
→ Daily Market State
→ Portfolio Construction / Execution

KISS remains the master portfolio-level allocation signal.

Daily Market State may identify:

- improving conditions;
- deteriorating conditions;
- changing risk appetite;
- narrowing or broadening participation;
- tactical risks;
- leadership changes;
- cross-asset confirmation;
- cross-asset contradiction;
- Macro Narrative confirmation;
- Macro Narrative contradiction;
- portfolio-specific implications.

Daily Market State must NEVER independently produce a portfolio-level KISS
buy/sell signal.

Daily Market State is a sentiment, market-temperature and pattern-observation
layer.


## 4. MACRO NARRATIVE DISTINCTION

### Macro Narrative

The Macro Narrative is:

- slow-moving;
- structural;
- based principally on supplied 42 Macro source material;
- governed by `manuals/macro-update-instructions.md`;
- intended to describe the prevailing economic, inflation, policy and
  liquidity environment.

The Macro Narrative changes only when governing macro evidence materially
changes the thesis.


### Daily Market State

Daily Market State is:

- fast-moving;
- market-derived;
- observational;
- intended to measure what financial markets are doing now.

Daily Market State may:

- CONFIRM the Macro Narrative;
- CONTRADICT the Macro Narrative;
- provide MIXED evidence;
- provide evidence that is NOT RELEVANT to the standing thesis.

Daily market observations do not automatically modify the Macro Narrative.

A Daily Market State contradiction may persist for multiple observations
without changing the structural Macro Narrative unless new governing macro
source material supports that change.

Never force market price action and the Macro Narrative to agree.


## 5. KISS DISTINCTION

KISS remains authoritative for portfolio-level asset allocation.

Daily Market State does not modify KISS.

Permitted language includes:

- no KISS implication;
- consistent with current KISS state;
- tactical evidence diverging from KISS;
- market deterioration worth monitoring;
- market conditions improving;
- KISS remains authoritative pending a KISS signal change.

Prohibited interpretations include:

- Daily Market State score implies KISS should sell;
- Daily Market State score implies KISS should buy;
- a detected pattern overrides KISS;
- market breadth independently changes strategic exposure;
- discretionary macro evidence independently changes strategic exposure.

Signals operating at different horizons are allowed to disagree.


## 6. OBSERVATION FREQUENCY

Produce ONE standard Daily Market State observation per completed US trading
day.

### NY CLOSE

Target generation time:

Approximately 07:00 GMT+7, after completion of the New York regular trading
session and after sufficient time for the TradingView data and visual capture
process to complete reliably.

Purpose:

- completed global trading-day record;
- completed US session;
- completed Singapore session;
- completed London session;
- definitive daily breadth and leadership snapshot;
- primary historical Daily Market State observation;
- primary input for the morning briefing.

Do NOT produce a separate NY OPEN Daily Market State observation.

Intraday market behavior may be discussed when specifically requested, but it
does not create another standardized Daily Market State row.


## 7. DATA LAYERS

The system distinguishes FAST market data from SLOW macro data.


### FAST DATA

May materially change each Daily Market State observation:

- broad-market price behavior;
- breadth;
- heatmaps;
- sectors;
- factors;
- relative-performance ratios;
- Treasury yields;
- bond-market volatility;
- credit;
- equity volatility;
- USD;
- gold;
- Bitcoin;
- regional leadership;
- portfolio market behavior.


### SLOW DATA

Changes less frequently:

- ISM / PMI;
- Macro Narrative;
- KISS regime/state;
- structural macro indicators;
- slower financial-condition indicators where applicable.

Slow data must not be described as newly changed merely because a new Daily
Market State observation is being produced.

Slow data provides context for fast-moving market evidence.


## 8. DAILY MARKET STATE VISUAL PACKAGE

The standardized Daily Market State visual package consists of THREE evidence
groups:

1. Market charts
2. Market heatmaps
3. Flagged watchlist screenshots

These evidence groups have different analytical purposes and should not be
treated as interchangeable.


## 9. MARKET CHARTS — PURPOSE

Generated market charts are reserved for signals where TREND, STRUCTURE,
MOMENTUM or PERSISTENCE adds substantial analytical information beyond the
one-day watchlist reading.

Do not generate charts merely because an instrument is interesting.

The permanent chart universe should remain deliberately small.

Individual sectors and most individual factors should normally remain
watchlist/heatmap evidence rather than becoming permanent generated charts.

## 11. STANDARDIZED RATIO ORIENTATION

Where analytically appropriate, relative-risk ratios should be constructed so
that:

UP = riskier, broader, more cyclical or economically sensitive asset
outperforming.

DOWN = defensive, narrower or less risk-seeking relative behavior.

Core standardized ratios:

IWD / IWF
= Value / Growth

SPHB / SPLV
= High Beta / Low Volatility

XLY / XLP
= Cyclicals / Defensives

IWM / MGC
= Small Cap / Mega Cap

RSP / SPY
= Equal Weight / Cap Weight

HYG / LQD
= High Yield / Investment Grade

HYG / SPY
= High Yield Credit / Equities

SPY / TLT
= Equities / Long-Duration Treasuries

EEM / SPY
= Emerging Markets / US Equities

Not every ratio is a pure risk-on/risk-off indicator.

In particular:

- EEM/SPY is primarily a global-leadership signal;
- SPY/TLT is affected by both equity and duration dynamics;
- HYG/SPY compares assets with materially different volatility;
- ratio interpretation must respect the underlying economic relationship.

Do not mechanically classify every rising ratio as bullish.

## 16. WATCHLIST SCREENSHOT COLUMN INTERPRETATION

This structure is HARD-CODED for Daily Market State analysis.

After NY close, TradingView flagged watchlist screenshots shall be interpreted
strictly as follows:

COLUMN 1
= Symbol / ticker

COLUMN 2
= Closing price

COLUMN 3
= Regular-session 1D percentage change

COLUMN 4, WHEN PRESENT
= Post-market percentage change


If three columns are visible:

Symbol | Closing Price | 1D Change %


If four columns are visible:

Symbol | Closing Price | 1D Change % | Post-Market Change %


Never confuse post-market movement with the completed regular-session move.

Post-market movement is NOT part of the completed regular-session Daily Market
State score.

Material post-market movement may be noted separately as forward information
for the following trading session.

Do not silently combine regular-session and post-market changes.

## 19. MARKET STATE COMPONENTS

Each observation evaluates exactly SEVEN market components.

Portfolio Alignment is evaluated separately and is NOT part of the Market
State Score.


### COMPONENT 1 — GROWTH / MACRO PRICING

Question:

Is market behavior consistent with strengthening or weakening nominal/real
growth expectations?

Evidence may include:

- cyclicals vs defensives;
- small vs mega;
- economically sensitive assets;
- commodities where relevant;
- financials / industrials / materials;
- current ISM/PMI context;
- regional growth-sensitive behavior.

Do not infer economic growth from one asset alone.


### COMPONENT 2 — RATES / FINANCIAL CONDITIONS

Question:

Are rates, Treasury volatility and financial conditions becoming more or less
supportive/stressful?

Evidence may include:

- US10Y;
- Treasury curve behavior where available;
- MOVE;
- NFCI;
- TLT;
- DXY where relevant.

Distinguish:

- orderly yield increases associated with stronger nominal growth;
- disorderly rate shocks;
- falling yields caused by benign disinflation;
- falling yields associated with growth fear.

Direction alone is insufficient.


### COMPONENT 3 — CREDIT

Question:

Is credit confirming or contradicting equity-market risk appetite?

Evidence may include:

- BAMLH0A0HYM2;
- HYG;
- LQD;
- HYG/LQD;
- HYG/SPY;
- credit-watchlist behavior;
- evidence of funding/credit stress.

Credit-spread evidence receives greater weight than simple one-day ETF price
moves where both are available.


### COMPONENT 4 — BREADTH / PARTICIPATION

Question:

Is market strength broadening or narrowing?

Primary evidence:

- S&P 500 heatmap;
- Nasdaq 100 heatmap;
- Dow heatmap;
- Russell 1000 heatmap;
- RSP/SPY;
- IWM/MGC;
- participation across market-cap tiers;
- concentration of gains/losses.

Headline index direction alone does not establish healthy breadth.


### COMPONENT 5 — FACTORS / LEADERSHIP

Question:

Which factor groups are gaining or losing leadership, and is leadership
consistent with increasing or decreasing risk appetite?

Core evidence includes the Four Horsemen:

- IWD/IWF — Value/Growth
- SPHB/SPLV — High Beta/Low Vol
- XLY/XLP — Cyclicals/Defensives
- IWM/MGC — Small/Mega

Additional factor-watchlist evidence may provide confirmation.

Do not require all Four Horsemen to move in the same direction.

Divergence among them is itself information.


### COMPONENT 6 — SECTORS

Question:

Is sector leadership consistent with broad risk appetite, defensive rotation,
growth concentration, reflationary broadening or other economically coherent
behavior?

Evaluate:

- Technology;
- Communication Services;
- Consumer Discretionary;
- Financials;
- Industrials;
- Energy;
- Materials;
- Health Care;
- Consumer Staples;
- Utilities;
- Real Estate.

Use heatmaps and watchlists as primary evidence.

Do not classify a market regime from a single sector move.


### COMPONENT 7 — GLOBAL / CROSS-ASSET

Question:

Is the market message internally consistent across major regions and asset
classes?

Evidence may include:

- United States;
- Europe;
- Singapore;
- Canada;
- Emerging Markets;
- DXY;
- gold;
- Bitcoin;
- bonds;
- commodities.

Global divergence is relevant evidence but is not automatically bearish or
bullish.


## 20. MARKET STATE SCORING

Each of the seven components receives one score:

+2 = strongly constructive / strongly risk-supportive
+1 = moderately constructive / supportive
 0 = neutral, balanced or genuinely mixed
-1 = moderately deteriorating / defensive
-2 = strongly deteriorating / stressed

Total Market State Score range:

-14 to +14


### IMPORTANT

The score measures MARKET STATE.

It does NOT measure:

- portfolio performance;
- KISS;
- expected future return;
- probability of a market top;
- probability of a market bottom.

Component scores must be determined from predefined evidence rules.

Do not score based on intuition, tone or narrative preference.

Do not convert missing evidence into zero.


## 21. MISSING DATA

Missing evidence is NOT neutral evidence.

If materially required evidence for a component is unavailable:

mark the component:

N/A

Do not assign 0 merely because data are missing.

If one or more components are N/A:

- report the available component scores;
- identify the missing component;
- state that total-score comparability is reduced;
- do not manufacture a full -14 to +14 score from incomplete evidence unless
  the governing scoring rules explicitly permit it.


## 22. TOTAL SCORE INTERPRETATION

The total score is descriptive, not a trading signal.

Suggested descriptive bands:

+9 to +14
= Strong Risk-On / Broadly Constructive

+4 to +8
= Risk-On / Constructive

+1 to +3
= Mildly Constructive

0
= Balanced / Mixed

-1 to -3
= Mildly Defensive

-4 to -8
= Risk-Off / Deteriorating

-9 to -14
= Strong Risk-Off / Stressed

The descriptive label does not override component-level evidence.

Two identical total scores may represent materially different market
structures.

Always preserve all seven component scores.


## 23. CHANGE / MOMENTUM

Each observation receives:

↑ IMPROVING
→ STABLE
↓ DETERIORATING

This describes the development of the component structure relative to recent
NY CLOSE observations.

Do not assign direction solely from the change in the total score.

Evaluate:

- number of components improving/deteriorating;
- magnitude of changes;
- persistence;
- breadth of confirmation;
- important cross-confirmations;
- emergence/fading of formal patterns.

Compare NY CLOSE with prior NY CLOSE observations only.

One-day noise should not automatically be labeled a developing trend.


## 24. FORMAL PATTERN PHILOSOPHY

Formal Daily Market State patterns must be:

- economically meaningful;
- observable from the standardized evidence set;
- sufficiently important to affect interpretation;
- defined before they occur;
- repeatable;
- resistant to narrative overfitting.

Do NOT create formal patterns for every interesting market configuration.

Do NOT invent a new pattern because recent price action appears unusual.

Formal patterns are descriptive market-state conditions, not predictions.

They do not call tops or bottoms.


## 25. FORMAL PATTERN SET

Version 1 contains SIX primary patterns.


### PATTERN 1 — BROAD RISK-ON / BROADENING

Concept:

Risk appetite is healthy and participation is expanding beyond narrow headline
index leadership.

Evidence should generally include multiple confirmations among:

- SPHB/SPLV improving;
- XLY/XLP improving;
- IWM/MGC improving;
- RSP/SPY improving;
- broad heatmap participation;
- healthy credit;
- broad sector participation.

Do not confirm from index gains alone.


### PATTERN 2 — NARROWING RISK-ON

Concept:

Headline equity indexes remain constructive while the quality and breadth of
participation deteriorate.

Evidence should generally include:

- constructive or resilient SPY/QQQ;
- weakening IWM/MGC and/or RSP/SPY;
- deteriorating breadth;
- increasing concentration in mega-cap/growth leadership;
- weakening SPHB/SPLV and/or XLY/XLP;
- possible loss of credit confirmation.

This pattern is NOT a market-top signal.

It indicates deterioration in the quality of participation.


### PATTERN 3 — DEFENSIVE ROTATION

Concept:

Leadership is shifting from cyclical/risk-seeking assets toward defensive
assets.

Evidence should generally include multiple confirmations among:

- XLY/XLP deteriorating;
- SPHB/SPLV deteriorating;
- defensive sectors gaining relative leadership;
- cyclicals losing leadership;
- deteriorating breadth;
- weaker small-cap participation.

Defensive Rotation may occur while headline indexes remain stable or rising.


### PATTERN 4 — CREDIT / FINANCIAL-STRESS DETERIORATION

Concept:

Credit, Treasury volatility and/or financial conditions are signaling
increasing systemic market stress.

Evidence should generally include meaningful deterioration among:

- BAMLH0A0HYM2;
- HYG/LQD;
- HYG;
- MOVE;
- NFCI;
- credit watchlist;
- Treasury-market behavior.

This pattern receives particular attention because credit/liquidity stress can
be more consequential than ordinary equity-sector rotation.

Do not confirm from one noisy daily ETF move alone.


### PATTERN 5 — GROWTH / REFLATION BROADENING

Concept:

Market leadership is broadening in a manner consistent with stronger nominal
growth and/or reflationary behavior.

Evidence should generally include multiple confirmations among:

- IWD/IWF improving;
- IWM/MGC improving;
- XLY/XLP improving;
- financials participating;
- industrials participating;
- materials participating;
- healthy credit;
- broad market participation;
- economically sensitive commodities behaving consistently where relevant.

This pattern may CONFIRM a standing reflation Macro Narrative.

It does not independently establish the macro regime.


### PATTERN 6 — RISK-OFF BROADENING

Concept:

Risk deterioration is becoming broad rather than isolated.

Evidence should generally include several of:

- multiple Four Horsemen deteriorating;
- broad heatmap weakness;
- deteriorating breadth;
- defensive leadership;
- weakening credit;
- rising VIX;
- rising MOVE;
- tightening financial conditions;
- synchronized regional weakness.

This pattern is not a market-bottom or crash prediction.

It describes broadening deterioration in current market conditions.


## 26. PATTERN STATES

Formal patterns may be assigned only one of:

EMERGING
CONFIRMED
STRENGTHENING
PERSISTENT
FADING
INVALIDATED

Pattern persistence must be evaluated using prior standardized Daily Market
State observations.

Do not call a pattern CONFIRMED from a single suggestive datapoint unless its
formal evidence threshold is clearly satisfied.

Do not use dramatic language when the evidence only supports EMERGING.


## 27. PATTERN EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

For every formal pattern reported, be able to identify:

- which standardized indicators support it;
- which standardized indicators contradict it;
- whether evidence strengthened or weakened versus prior observations;
- whether the condition is new or persistent.

Contradictory evidence must not be hidden.

A pattern with significant conflicting evidence should remain EMERGING or
FADING rather than being forced into CONFIRMED.


## 28. MACRO ALIGNMENT

Each observation compares current Daily Market State evidence against the
standing Macro Narrative.

Use only:

CONFIRMING
MIXED
CONTRADICTING
NOT RELEVANT

Macro Alignment is NOT a numerical score.

Explain the classification in one concise sentence.

Market evidence may contradict the Macro Narrative without changing the Macro
Narrative.

Do not force short-term market behavior to validate the structural thesis.


## 29. PORTFOLIO ALIGNMENT — SEPARATE FROM MARKET SCORE

Portfolio Alignment is evaluated separately from the seven-component Market
State Score.

It must NOT affect the -14 to +14 Market State Score.

Portfolio Alignment score:

+2 = market environment strongly favorable/confirming for major current
     portfolio exposures/theses

+1 = moderately favorable/confirming

 0 = mixed / neutral

-1 = moderately unfavorable/diverging

-2 = materially unfavorable/contradicting important portfolio exposures/theses

Portfolio Alignment measures the relationship between CURRENT MARKET
CONDITIONS and the ACTUAL PORTFOLIO.

It does not measure whether the portfolio made or lost money that day.


## 30. PORTFOLIO RELEVANCE — REQUIRED SOURCES

Before producing portfolio-specific Daily Market State commentary:

1. Read current Holdings.
2. Read Portfolio Summary.
3. Read KISS State.
4. Check Notes and Sync Metadata freshness.
5. Read Exposure.
6. Read relevant Stock Analysis where useful.
7. Review relevant current technical charts where technically material.
8. Consult current holding thesis/risk/invalidation information where material.

Holdings establishes direct ownership.

Exposure establishes known look-through exposure.

Stock Analysis is supplemental and never establishes ownership.

The Holdings report may provide security thesis, risks/invalidation, ETF
composition and other reference information where required.

Do not infer current ownership from:

- watchlists;
- Daily Market State screenshots;
- Stock Analysis;
- Macro Narrative;
- prior conversations;
- memory.


## 31. PORTFOLIO RELEVANCE — ANALYTICAL QUESTIONS

Portfolio commentary should evaluate, where materially relevant:

- Is the market environment supportive of the portfolio's major exposures?
- Are portfolio themes participating in or diverging from broader leadership?
- Are current holdings behaving consistently with their intended portfolio
  role?
- Is weakness broad-market, regional, sector-specific or security-specific?
- Are known look-through concentrations increasing the relevance of a market
  development?
- Does current market behavior interact with an existing holding thesis?
- Does it interact with a known thesis risk or invalidation condition?
- Does it materially change execution risk?
- Is apparent diversification being undermined by correlated underlying
  exposure?
- Are Singapore, income/value, quality, broad equity, growth/technology,
  Canada or other actual portfolio clusters being affected?

Do not manufacture ticker commentary when no meaningful relationship exists.

Do not convert ordinary daily price movement into thesis deterioration.


## 32. EXPOSURE LIMITATIONS

Exposure is the authoritative current derived look-through dataset.

Respect its limitations:

- known look-through exposure may be incomplete;
- missing exposure is not zero;
- fund reference sheets may contain only top holdings;
- unresolved exposure may exist;
- Exposure totals need not equal invested assets.

When look-through coverage is incomplete, portfolio conclusions based on
Exposure must be framed as known/lower-bound exposure rather than complete
economic exposure.

## 39. DATA FRESHNESS

Before every Daily Market State observation verify:

- Daily Market State visual-package timestamp;
- Portfolio Live State freshness;
- Exposure freshness when used;
- Stock Analysis freshness when used;
- relevant portfolio chart freshness when used;
- current Macro Narrative;
- current KISS state.

Fresh synchronization does not imply fresh underlying source data.

If evidence is stale, identify the limitation before materially relying on it.


## 40. FAILURE PROTOCOL

If the Daily Market State visual package is incomplete:

- do not pretend it is complete;
- do not reconstruct missing screenshots from memory;
- identify affected evidence;
- mark affected scores N/A where necessary;
- reduce confidence/comparability.

If Portfolio Live State is unavailable:

- do not produce portfolio-specific conclusions.

If Exposure is stale/unavailable:

- report the limitation;
- do not reconstruct look-through exposure from memory.

If Stock Analysis is required but stale/unavailable:

- report the limitation;
- do not invent values.

If current KISS state is unavailable:

- do not infer it.

If the Macro Narrative is unavailable:

- omit Macro Alignment rather than reconstructing the thesis from memory.


## 41. ANALYTICAL DISCIPLINE

Always distinguish:


### OBSERVATION

What the standardized market evidence directly shows.


### INTERPRETATION

What the combination of evidence plausibly means.


### FORMAL PATTERN

A predefined rule-based condition supported by standardized evidence.


### MACRO ALIGNMENT

Whether current market evidence confirms, contradicts or is mixed relative to
the standing structural Macro Narrative.


### PORTFOLIO ALIGNMENT

How the current market environment relates to actual current portfolio
exposures, theses and risks.


### KISS

The authoritative portfolio-level allocation signal.


Never collapse these categories into one conclusion.


## 42. LANGUAGE DISCIPLINE

Avoid unsupported predictive language.

Do not say:

- "the market is topping";
- "the bottom is in";
- "this guarantees a correction";
- "the market will crash";
- "this proves the bull market continues."

Prefer:

- participation is narrowing;
- credit is no longer confirming;
- risk appetite is improving;
- defensive leadership is strengthening;
- deterioration has persisted for three observations;
- current evidence is mixed;
- the pattern remains emerging;
- the Macro Narrative remains intact but market confirmation has weakened.

Observation takes precedence over prediction.
